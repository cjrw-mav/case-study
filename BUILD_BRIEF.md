# Build Brief — Nourish Care Operations Director Case Study

## 1. Objective

Build a polished, interactive web-based case-study presentation for an Operations Director interview at Nourish Care.

This is not a conventional website and should not feel like a generic personal portfolio.

It is an executive facilitation artifact that must work in two ways:

### Presentation Mode
Used live during a 45-minute panel case-study session via screen share.

Requirements:
- Large, clean, presentation-friendly layouts
- Minimal text on each screen
- Clear narrative progression
- Keyboard navigation
- Smooth transitions
- Interactive elements used selectively
- No unnecessary scrolling during the core presentation
- Must be reliable enough to present live

### Explore Mode
Used by the panel after the interview via a shared URL.

Requirements:
- Same core narrative
- Additional context and detail available progressively
- Expandable examples / evidence
- Experience library
- Assumptions and supporting logic
- Clear enough to understand without the presenter

The site should feel like a cross between:
- an executive presentation
- a lightweight operating-system prototype
- a polished strategy artifact

It should NOT feel like:
- a marketing website
- a portfolio/CV website
- a dashboard
- an AI gimmick
- a consulting slide deck simply rebuilt in HTML


---

# 2. Primary message

Everything should reinforce one core proposition:

> Scale customer impact faster than organisational complexity.

Supporting narrative:

Nourish is moving from roughly £30m+ revenue toward ~£120m while simultaneously needing materially stronger operating leverage.

The challenge is not simply to grow.

It is to:
- increase customer impact
- improve operating margin
- prevent complexity from compounding
- use AI and technology to release human capacity
- preserve mission, agility and humanity as the company scales

The operating philosophy underneath the artifact is:

> Better operations release human capacity.
> Better use of technology directs that capacity toward higher-value work.
> Better customer value ultimately supports better care.

The "Tech for Good" theme should run subtly through the entire experience rather than appearing only on an opening or closing slide.

- enterprise priorities
- operating rhythm

### £60–90m

- leadership bandwidth

Operating response:
- portfolio governance
- repeatable programme capability
- integration playbooks
- capacity planning

### £90–120m
Headline:

- margin expansion pressure
- people costs scaling too closely with revenue
- incremental optimisation no longer sufficient
- duplicate capability
- fragmented automation

Operating response:
- organisational simplification
- AI-native workflows
- structural automation
- measurable capacity release

# 12. Scene 07 — Mobilise & Scale

This covers Section 2 of the case study.

Title:

"Build enough central capability to optimise the enterprise — not another silo."

Three major areas.

## What I need from Nourish
- clear sponsorship
- access to people/data/customers
- explicit decision rights
- willingness to challenge existing norms
- executive reinforcement of agreed priorities

## How the function scales

Show a federated model.

Central Enterprise Ops core could eventually contain:
- Business Operations / Performance
- Transformation / PMO
- Process Excellence
- internal AI enablement / automation
- enterprise tooling

Functional Ops stays close to functions:
- RevOps
- Product Ops
- Customer Ops
- People Ops

Principle:

"Centralise the mechanisms that optimise the enterprise; keep specialist operations close to the functions."

Avoid implying all Ops teams must report directly to the Operations Director.

## Two biggest challenges

Challenge 1:
UNBOUNDED MANDATE

Risk:
The role becomes the person who personally fixes every cross-functional problem.

Response:
Build mechanisms and capability, not dependency.

Challenge 2:
STRUCTURE WITHOUT ADOPTION

Risk:
Processes/governance are designed but never become organisational behaviour.

Response:
Co-create, build capability, create social proof, measure use and remove mechanisms that do not create value.
